About

I am a physicist and Professor at the Federal University of Piauí (UFPI), Brazil. My research focuses on Relativistic Quantum Information, Black Hole Physics, and Quantum Field Theory in Curved Spacetimes.

I hold a Bachelor's and Master's degree in Physics from UFPI and a PhD in Sciences from the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG). I am also a member of the International Society for Relativistic Quantum Information (ISRQi).

Research

01

Relativistic Quantum Information

Quantum information in non-inertial systems, accelerated detectors, Unruh physics, coherence, entanglement, and relativistic quantum protocols.

02

Black Hole Physics

Quantum and classical aspects of black holes, wave scattering, quasinormal modes, shadows, Hawking radiation, and black-hole observables.

03

QFT in Curved Spacetime

Quantum fields in expanding and curved backgrounds, particle creation, vacuum structure, and quantum correlations in dynamical spacetimes.

04

Quantum Technologies

Quantum metrology, interferometry, coherence-based sensing, and educational approaches connecting fundamental physics with quantum technologies.
